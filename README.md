  # Call
  
  **A minimalist, immutable, offline terminal log for Android.**<br>
  *No cloud. No algorithms. Just pure existence.*
</div>

---

## ✨ Core Features

-  **Absolute Sovereignty**: No network permissions, no tracking, no accounts. Your data lives and dies on your physical device.
-  **Immutable by Design**: Once you exit, the text is sealed. There is no "edit" button. You cannot alter the past.
-  **Zero Interference**: No push notifications, no daily streaks, no AI auto-complete. Just you and the blinking cursor.
-  **Dual Modes**: Switch seamlessly between **Paper Mode** (bone-white) and **Terminal Mode** (pitch-black).
-  **Pure Markdown**: Every thought is saved as a raw `.md` file directly in your local sandbox.
